about me ↗
I’m a Project Manager who enjoys making complicated work feel manageable. I work at the intersection of people, process, and delivery — turning ambiguous briefs into workable plans, building structure around moving pieces, and adapting when reality changes.

I’ve grown from learning project management and creative production from the ground up to managing complex, high-volume creative delivery. Along the way, I’ve built systems for planning, estimation, training, reporting, and documentation — always with the goal of making work more predictable and easier to scale.

What makes my approach different is how I balance data with reality. I use project data and historical performance to inform decisions, while recognizing that projects are ultimately run by people, priorities, and context.
